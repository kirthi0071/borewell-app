Privacy Policy — Borewell Manager

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Borewell Manager ("the app") is a business tool used by borewell service companies to manage jobs, customers, bills and staff. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

Operated by: [Your business name], [Address], India. Contact: support@example.com

What we collect

  • Login details: the business owner's name and mobile number; staff names and usernames created by the owner. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Business records entered by users: customer names, phone numbers, villages/addresses, job and borewell details (depth, water level, casing, motor), bills, payments and expenses.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising IDs or analytics.

How it is used

Only to run the app for your business: showing jobs, calculating bills, creating PDF bills and preparing WhatsApp messages that you choose to send. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), encrypted in transit and at rest. Each business's data is separated: logins of one business cannot read another business's data. Inside a business, the owner (admin) controls what office staff and crew can see.

Sharing

Data is shared only with Google Firebase as our hosting provider. Bills or messages are shared to WhatsApp or other apps only when a user taps Share.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until deleted by the business. Users can delete their account inside the app: Account menu (👤) → Delete account. When the admin deletes their account, all of that business's data is deleted and staff logins are disabled. You can also request deletion by emailing the contact above from the registered business; we will complete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We will update this page if the policy changes.
